Connection problems

Tell us which AI host you use, the approximate time, and the error message. Do not send passwords, access tokens, private connection keys, or your full notebook.

For an account mismatch, check the OneDrive account authorized for that specific AI connection. Logging into the website does not switch another AI connection.

Control your data

Website logout ends the browser session. Disconnecting OneDrive in the editor revokes that account’s service access. Neither action deletes the Markdown files in your OneDrive or chat history held by your AI provider.

Delete unwanted notes directly in OneDrive and request an index rebuild if needed. For access or deletion requests concerning service-held data, contact the email above.
