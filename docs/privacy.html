What we process and why

Shared Notebook reads and writes Markdown memories in the OneDrive account you authorize. We process your Microsoft account identifier, available name and email, OAuth access and refresh tokens, and the titles, tags, note contents, summaries and search queries supplied to the service. We use these to authenticate connections, select the correct notebook, fulfill requests and maintain the notebook index.

Microsoft handles your Microsoft password; our service does not provide a form to collect it. We do not automatically collect your entire AI chat history. Only content sent through the connector or editor is processed. Please do not submit passwords, access tokens, payment secrets or unnecessary sensitive information.

We do not sell memory data, use it for advertising, or use it to train separate models. The AI host and other providers have their own policies for data they receive.

Where data goes

Original Markdown notes are stored in your OneDrive. Requests are processed through our Cloudflare connection gateway and AXHub-hosted service, Microsoft Graph, and the AI provider you choose to connect. These services may process data outside your country under their own terms. This is not end-to-end encryption between you and the AI: the service must process requested content.

Service authentication records are encrypted with AES-256-GCM at rest. A legacy personal-key webhook path may cache an encrypted successful tool response with a five-minute validity period to prevent duplicate execution. Expired records cannot be used, but physical removal occurs during subsequent cleanup.

Retention

Browser sessions are valid for seven days, service AI access tokens for one hour, and AI refresh tokens and personal connection keys for thirty days. Sign-in or renewal can issue a new validity period. Microsoft connection records are retained until disconnection. OAuth client-registration records do not expire automatically. You may request removal of service-held personal data by email.

OneDrive files remain until you delete them. Provider-managed logs and backups are subject to provider policies and operational settings; expiry or disconnection does not promise immediate removal from all backups. For questions about retention or deletion, contact the operator.

Your controls

You can read and edit memories in the editor or your OneDrive. Website logout ends that browser session; it does not disconnect AI clients. Disconnecting a OneDrive account in the editor removes its service connection record and blocks existing service access for that account. It does not delete OneDrive notes or AI-host chat history.

Delete unwanted notes in OneDrive and rebuild the index if needed. Microsoft account permissions can also be revoked through Microsoft. For access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests about service-held data, email forestmoon@gmail.com. We may need to verify account ownership. Never email a password, authentication token or full private notebook.

Audience and changes

This service is for general users who meet the age and consent requirements of their AI host, Microsoft and applicable law. It is not directed to children under thirteen. Do not connect an account or provide data if you do not meet these requirements.

Policy changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Contact the operator with questions.
